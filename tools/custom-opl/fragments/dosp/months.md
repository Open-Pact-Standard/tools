In addition to the abandonment conversion in §5, each **Version** of the Work
converts to the **Apache License 2.0** exactly **N months** after that Version's
first public release date, where N is the DOSP period declared in `NOTICE`. The
conversion is automatic and applies to the source of that Version as published.
Existing commercial licensees keep their rights for the Version they licensed;
DOSP opens the source only to new adopters after the DOSP date. (N may be any
positive integer; values beyond the 24-60 recommended range are permitted for
long-horizon projects.)
